Dimension-based rectangles, not traced room outlines. Actual shape and usable area may differ. Closets, bays and alcoves are not separately measured.

What you’re comparing.

These are published room dimensions from university housing descriptions, converted to feet and drawn at one shared scale. The area is length × width. It is an estimate of that rectangle, not a measured usable floor area or space per person.

Only rooms with one unambiguous pair of dimensions are included. Gray Court’s published default for doubles is included and labeled; the university qualifies it as “unless otherwise noted.” Rooms with added sections, irregular measurements, missing units, or obvious dimension errors are left out. Explicitly identified RA/staff and accessible rooms are also excluded from this comparison. A missing room or hall means we cannot make this particular comparison from the records we have.

Housing assignments, room configurations and availability can change. Use the linked source and current housing selection materials before making your list.
