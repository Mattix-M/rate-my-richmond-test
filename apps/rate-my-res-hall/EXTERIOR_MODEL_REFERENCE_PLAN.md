# Recognizable residence exterior model reference plan

Research pass: 2026-10-19. Scope: all 19 housing groups in `data/housing/index.json`. These are reference links for original illustrative geometry, not a collection of licensed textures or measured elevation drawings. Search previews and indexed source text were available; several Richmond pages returned consent redirects or fetch errors on direct opening. Those limitations are explicit below.

## Build direction

The target is recognizable architecture at interaction scale: footprint, roof silhouette, brick/stone/timber balance, window rhythm and entrance treatment. Preserve those identifiers before adding decorative detail. The footprint can come from the existing floor drawings; exterior photos supply appearance. A floor sheet is not evidence of exterior height or precise window elevation.

Use six reusable families, with building-specific footprints and roof pieces:

1. **Forest townhouse:** compact brick rows, pitched roofs, light trim, small entrance canopies. Start with UFA 166, then vary row lengths without inventing letter positions.
2. **Gateway apartment:** taller apartment volumes, pitched roof, bay projections and porch entry; four occupied floor choices, following the official housing description.
3. **Historic Tudor dorm:** brick lower walls, light infill/dark timber accents, dormers and gables; Jeter, Thomas, Wood and Robins need individual roof/footprint variants.
4. **Later Tudor/Gothic hall:** simpler repeated windows and restrained trim, footprint-specific wings; Moore, Marsh, Gray and possibly Dennis/Freeman after photo confirmation.
5. **Courtyard Gothic:** North Court deserves its own courtyard/arch/tower silhouette. Westhampton, South and Keller can share materials but should not reuse North's whole mesh.
6. **Small house:** Atlantic/Pacific require a separate domestic scale; exterior appearance is not yet sufficiently verified.

Suggested interaction: begin with a compact assembled building, separate floors only a little on exploration, lift the roof aside, then rotate the selected floor into its readable drawing. Do not put miniature drawings or large text strips on building roofs. A missing upper-level plan should remain a labeled detail view rather than a fabricated full apartment floor.

## UFA plan chronology caution

The current official apartment page says the UFAs were renovated during **2014–2017**, adding open communal space and half bathrooms. Therefore an old source sheet labeled “proposed” is not, by itself, evidence of a renovation planned years from now. The user explicitly requested removing proposed panels from the interface; respect that display choice. Do not label the retained “original” diagram as verified present-day configuration without further confirmation. This does not block the recognizable exterior prototype or improved interaction.
