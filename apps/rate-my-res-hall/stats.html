    A FEW OTHER THINGS

    A few numbers. A few things you probably didn’t know.

    What these numbers mean.

    The capacities come from university housing pages. They tell you how many residents a place accommodates, not who lives there today, which rooms are free or your housing lottery odds. Missing figures stay missing. Construction and opening dates can mean different things.

    Sources are linked. Some floor plans and room records are incomplete, so adding them up would give us a very confident wrong answer. We use published capacity instead.
