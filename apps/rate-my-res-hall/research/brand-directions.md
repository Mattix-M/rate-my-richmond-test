# Brand directions — naming exploration

Concepts, not a selected name. No live branding changed. Domain, social handle and trademark availability are unchecked.

Open `brand-directions.svg` for three original vector directions, each with an icon, compact header and 1200 × 630 link-preview composition.

## 1. The Room List

The list a student actually makes before their housing selection time. Plain language rather than a manufactured platform name. The plan-shaped mark represents rooms and comparisons without repeating the current house badge. Possible drawback: a common phrase is harder to distinguish in search, so it needs the Richmond subline.

Subline: **Richmond housing, before your pick.**

## 2. Before You Pick

Describes when the guide is useful. Feels conversational, and could support practical advice, comparisons and reviews without claiming to be an official housing system. The bracket-and-tick icon makes a choice feel concrete. Possible drawback: the name needs its Richmond housing subline to establish context.

Subline: **A Richmond housing guide.**

## 3. Richmond Roombook

Positions the site as a useful reference for students building a shortlist. The open book and red saved-page ribbon connect information with saving rooms. Keeps the serif style of the hall titles with a distinct visual identity. Possible drawback: “room book” might be mistaken for a booking service unless the supporting text is clear.

Subline: **Your shortlist starts here.**

## More names to react to

These are rough options, not claims that the names are available:

- **Room for Richmond** — more campus-specific and warm.
- **The Housing List** — direct, with room for notes and comparisons.
- **Richmond, Room by Room** — descriptive editorial title.
- **Pick Your Place** — friendly action, broad enough for future features.
- **Roomside** — short and softer, needs a campus subline.
- **Your Next Room** — student-facing and immediately understandable.
- **Hall Notes** — supports student-written observations and sourced facts.
- **The Hall Guide** — clear about the content, less distinctive.
- **Room Check** — practical due diligence before choosing.
- **Before Move-In** — useful feeling, though later in the journey than selection.
- **The Richmond Shortlist** — best for a comparison/saved-options product.

The choice should establish what the site helps with first. A name that sounds good but feels like a room-booking portal would add confusion. All three board directions preserve the white/navy/blue/brick-red palette and serif headings, while avoiding another generic red house tile.
