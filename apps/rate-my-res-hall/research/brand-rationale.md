## Mark

A red rounded tile holds a white architectural floor-plan mark. Its open door arc connects the brand to rooms and floor plans, and its simple silhouette holds up at tab-icon size. The icon is original vector artwork; favicon PNG/ICO and Apple touch variants are generated from that source.
