How we counted & what these numbers mean

Residents per washer = published resident capacity ÷ listed washers. This assumes full occupancy. It does not predict wait times, current occupancy or access to another hall’s laundry room.

Machine counts were read from the Speed Queen app on October 22, 2026, including machines marked offline or error. They are app entries, not an independently verified physical inventory. Check Speed Queen for current availability.

*Marsh lists two separate entries named “Dryer 13.” Both are included in its 10 dryer entries; we haven’t verified whether these represent two physical dryers.

The UFA comparison combines three laundry locations. The number of residents assigned to each laundry room is unknown. Atlantic and Pacific weren’t listed separately, so their counts stay unknown. Robins and Wood have counts but no verified capacity.
