What these tours show

Each tour shows an example unit. It may differ from your room, floor, or building. Labels describe the linked tour, not every layout in the hall. Atlantic and Pacific Houses are not listed in the university’s tour directory.

Tours are presented by the University of Richmond, produced by Vivid Media, and use Matterport. They open on the original provider’s website. Our editable planner includes reconstructions of these example units. Their proportions, fixtures and furniture are visual estimates, not measured scans.

All 17 tours were reviewed on October 22, 2026. Tour screenshots and observation notes are saved as local research references.
