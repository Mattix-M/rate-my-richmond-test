# UFA exterior reference research

Checked 2026-10-19 for the UFA 166 interaction prototype. These are reference links, not image assets licensed or copied into the app.

## Verified building and unit facts

- [Official Residence Life apartment page](https://residencelife.richmond.edu/housing/upperclass/ufa-gateway.html) explicitly lists **166A as a one-level apartment**, along with 160F, 164H and 486A. Other UFAs use the two-level apartment type. This is an important exception to a universal two-story-unit model.
- [Official residential address crosswalk](https://engage.richmond.edu/service-action/elections/on-campus-residential-addresses.pdf) lists **166 A–H UR Drive** as the new address for former **500–507 UFA**. It establishes the letters and block identity, but does not locate each letter on the building.
- [Official campus map](https://www.richmond.edu/visit/maps/print/campusmap.pdf) and [Facilities address directory](https://facilities.richmond.edu/about-us/campus-addresses/index.html) support campus placement and the UFA address groups. They do not provide façade elevations.

## Exterior reference candidates

1. [Milestone Construction portfolio](https://www.milestonecc.net/about-1). Its University of Richmond entry explicitly includes **Forest Apartments Phase V** and links a townhouse exterior image. The image-search preview shows two-story red-brick rows, dark pitched roofs, small gabled entrance canopies, light trim, and mature trees. This supports a general UFA architectural vocabulary, **not an identified image of block 166**.

   - Full image fetch was unavailable through the research browser; the image-search preview was available. Verify the full image before measuring details.
2. [The Collegian: Students discover pests in campus housing](https://www.thecollegianur.com/article/2022/09/students-discover-pests-in-campus-housing), September 22, 2022. The [author archive](https://www.thecollegianur.com/staff/tyler-rosenstein) identifies its photo as “University Forest Apartments.” The image-search preview shows brick buildings with small porch roofs along a landscaped, wooded walkway. The caption does not identify 166.
   - Image reference: `https://snworksceo.imgix.net/urc/cce97064-8d1b-4581-8566-7b2b2298ab24.sized-1000x1000.jpg`
3. [Official September 2023 family newsletter](https://residencelife.richmond.edu/residence-life/Connections/SpiderFamilyNewsletterSeptemberEdition2023.pdf) includes a UFA hall highlight and event photo. Useful for setting/context; not an elevation or unit-location source.

## Modeling boundary

We can use the references to make a recognizable, clearly illustrative brick-townhouse prototype, with pitched roof and porch accents. Current research does **not** verify block 166's exact front/rear elevations, roof geometry, window counts, heights, or the spatial order of 166A–H. In particular, do not assign 166A a second floor or infer unit-letter positions from the old number sequence.

For an accurate block model, the best next evidence is a clearly identified photo of 166 from the front, one oblique/rear angle, and an aerial footprint. Street View screenshots supplied by the user would help only if public references do not resolve those details. No user images are required to implement and test the continuous camera/slice/plan interaction.
