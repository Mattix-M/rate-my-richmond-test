How your matches work

We check published year and program rules first, then weight the features you care about. Your two priorities count three times as much as your other preferences. There are no personality labels or guessed dorm reputations.

Distances are straight-line distances between campus-map pins, not walking routes. Laundry ratios use published capacity and the machines recorded on October 22, 2026; they do not show occupancy or live availability. Missing data stays unknown.

Room types, assignment rules, and availability can change. A result is a place to investigate, not a reserved room or a guarantee of eligibility. First-year assignments and residential programs may determine your hall.
