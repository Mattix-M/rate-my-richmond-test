How the numbers work.

The source

12,239 anonymous Rate My Professors reviews, spanning 2002–2026. Reviewers are self-selected. These are opinions, not a representative student survey or an official University of Richmond evaluation. Anonymous comments may contain unverified claims.

Ranking without the one-review miracle

The all-time rankings use the supplied 219 eligible professors with at least 20 reviews. Bayesian shrinkage pulls quality and difficulty toward the Richmond-wide average, with a prior weight of 20 reviews. Best and worst use adjusted quality; hardest and easiest use adjusted difficulty.

The recent window

Recent means 2022 through 2026, inclusive. Adjusted rankings require 15 recent reviews and use a prior weight of 10 reviews. We calculate them from the source reviews using that documented formula and reproduce all three official recent award scores exactly. Recent Alpha was not supplied. 2026 is a partial year.

Professor Alpha

A custom metric, not an official RMP score. Alpha asks whether ratings are higher or lower than expected after accounting for reported difficulty, department, and review year. Positive means higher than expected. The site displays the supplied shrunk Alpha. It does not measure teaching effectiveness.

Polarization

The standard deviation of quality ratings. A higher value means more disagreement, not necessarily a worse professor. The all-time award follows the supplied official winner.

Departments and schools

Headline department and academic group values come directly from the supplied summaries. Economics stays separate from Robins. Broad STEM adds Math & CS to Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. Review-weighted means treat reviews equally; professor-weighted means treat professors equally.

Missing results stay missing

No recent department summary, recent Alpha, complete difficulty–quality regression, or within-professor time model was supplied. We show available descriptive evidence and label missing model details. The approximately −0.41 difficulty relationship comes from the brief, not a newly fitted regression.

Names, reviews, and curated humor

Fourteen explicit alias mappings reconcile canonical names to the supplied counts and means. All original comments are preserved exactly. Funny labels use only 30 review keys and exact comments verified against the supplied Master Top 30 curation sheet. No Unhinged labels are inferred.

Coverage and interpretation

There are 877 professor name groups after the documented alias joins. Some source departments have no supplied academic mapping and remain “Unmapped.” The 27 headline department summaries do not cover the full review corpus. Their professor counts may use source identities before alias merging. Unknown or ineligible adjusted scores appear as a dash. Source CSVs are never changed by the app’s preprocessing script.

The Robins regression outputs use course-level and review-year controls. A 95% confidence interval describes estimate uncertainty under the model assumptions. A p-value measures compatibility with a zero-difference model; it is not the probability a conclusion is true. These are associations in self-selected reviews, not causal findings.
