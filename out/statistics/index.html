What goes into Alpha?

Professor Alpha is a custom metric: how much higher or lower students rate a professor than expected for their difficulty, department, and review year. Positive means higher than expected. It is not an official RMP score. This visualization uses the supplied shrunk Alpha, which pulls estimates toward zero to reduce small-sample extremes. All-time eligible professors have at least 20 reviews. The model coefficients and recent Alpha estimates were not supplied. Alpha is about reported satisfaction, not teaching effectiveness.
